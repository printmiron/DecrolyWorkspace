1. Oral Presentation Script (What you can say)

Slide 1 – Title: “My First Day at Work”
Hello everyone. Today I’m going to talk about my first day at work.
I just started a new job this week as a web developer.

Slide 2 – My Job and Workplace
I work in a small tech company with a friendly and young team.
The office is modern and bright, which makes it a nice place to work.
I met my supervisor, Alex, and my teammate, Paul. They are very helpful and kind.

Slide 3 – My Tasks and Responsibilities
On my first day, we had a short morning meeting to talk about projects.
Then I created a simple web page using HTML and CSS to show my skills.
I also learned to use GitHub, which the company uses to share code.

Slide 4 – About Me as a Professional
I am a motivated and curious person.
I like learning new things and working in a team.
My main strengths are problem-solving and creativity.

Slide 5 – My Experience and Background
Before this job, I studied web development and took some online courses in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
This job is a great opportunity to get real experience.

Slide 6 – What I Enjoy
I really enjoy creating websites and seeing my code come to life.
It’s exciting to build something that other people can use.

Slide 7 – My Interests
In my free time, I enjoy playing video games and watching tech tutorials online.
These hobbies help me stay creative and improve my skills.

Slide 8 – Conclusion
My first day was exciting and positive.
Even though I was a little nervous at the beginning, now I feel more confident.
I’m sure I will learn a lot in this job.
